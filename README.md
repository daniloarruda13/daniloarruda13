I am a data scientist and researcher with a Ph.D. in kinesiology. My work connects
human movement, sports performance, virtual reality, and practical software: from
cleaning complex experimental data to building predictive models and interactive
tools that make results easier to understand and use.

## Current focus

- Human-centered data science for movement, ergonomics, and performance.
- Reproducible biomechanics and time-series analysis in Python, R, and MATLAB.
- Machine-learning workflows that keep preprocessing and evaluation leakage-safe.
- Interactive analytics with Plotly, Streamlit, Power BI, and R Shiny.
- Virtual-reality research on perception, action, and cybersickness.
